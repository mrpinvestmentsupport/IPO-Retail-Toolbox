Concept 01

Fresh Capital vs. OFS

Where does your IPO bid money go upon successful allotment?

Click to flip card

The Core Difference:

Fresh Issue: New shares created. Money enters the company balance sheet.
OFS: Zero new shares. Old promoters cash out their personal holdings.
Always prefer companies with high Fresh Issue allocations.

Why retail investors must never bid below the cap price.

Concept 03

Grey Market Premium (GMP)

Is unofficial grey market trading a reliable listing indicator?

Click to flip card

Reality Check:

GMP is completely unofficial and unregulated. While it reflects informal market sentiment, it can be manipulated by syndicates and fluctuate wildly overnight.

⚠️ Never base investment decisions purely on social media GMP rumors.
Focus on underlying financials and valuation instead.
Concept 04

Anchor Investor Lock-in

What happens when anchor investor lock-in periods expire?

Click to flip card

Supply Unlock Windows:

Anchor shares are locked in two tranches: 50% for 30 days and 50% for 90 days post-listing.

📈 Watch out for potential stock price volatility around these calendar dates.
Institutional supply release test.
Concept 05

DRHP vs. RHP Documents

Understanding the paperwork filed with SEBI and exchanges.

Click to flip card

Document Stages:

DRHP (Draft): Submitted months in advance for SEBI review & public feedback.
RHP (Red Herring): Finalized prospectus without price, published right before opening.
Check RHP risk factors section.
Concept 06

Oversubscription Lottery

How allotment is decided when retail quota is heavily oversubscribed.

Click to flip card

Allotment Mechanics:

If retail is 20x oversubscribed, applying for 10 lots does not increase your probability. Every PAN gets a single lottery ticket for 1 minimum lot.

🎯 Apply via multiple family member PANs to increase overall chances!
SEBI computerized random allocation.
